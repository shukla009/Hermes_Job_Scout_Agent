# Free alert setup (one-time account action)

- The scout can read authenticated alerts in Gmail's `HermesJobAlerts` label. This does not subscribe you to any platform automatically.
- In your existing candidate accounts, create daily email alerts, then filter those alert messages into that label. No paid plan, premium promotion or paid training is needed.
- Start with LinkedIn and Indeed: research/market intelligence and junior analyst searches in Delhi-NCR, Noida and Gurgaon/Gurugram. Keep a separate India-remote search.
- Use full-time filters and entry-level/associate filters where available. Do not filter for your salary in an outreach email.
- Suggested search groups:
  - Research Analyst, Research Associate, Market Research Analyst, Business Research Analyst, Market Intelligence, Competitive Intelligence, Strategy Analyst.
  - Process Analyst, Operations Analyst, Business Operations Analyst, Reporting Analyst, MIS Analyst, Data Quality Analyst.
  - Junior Data Analyst, BI Analyst, Power BI Analyst (only genuine requirement matches).
- A platform's normal job alert may be daily, not immediate. It cannot establish original publication time. The scout still verifies the actual employer vacancy and rejects unknown/older-than-48-hour dates.
- Do not auto-forward private conversations or newsletters into the label. Only authenticated allowed sender domains are read; no attachment downloads or LinkedIn login scraping.

## Candidate-channel assessment

| Platform | Intended use | Limitation / zero-spend rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| LinkedIn | Native daily alerts; manual referrals and applications | No logged-in scraping or automated messaging; up to 20 native alerts. |
| Indeed | Native keyword/location email alerts | Availability depends on account/location; employer evidence must survive tracking links. |
| Foundit | Relevant candidate alerts/profile matching | Use free candidate functions only; no paid resume or visibility product. |
| Hirist | Selective analyst/BI roles | Many listings require more experience; no Pro or premium feature. |
| iimjobs | Selective business research/analyst opportunities | Many roles require MBA/experienced candidates. Pro membership is paid and excluded. |
| Instahyre | Free candidate profile and relevant notifications | Best treated as supplementary; no login automation or assumed open application API. |
| Cutshort | Selective data/analytics and operations roles | Tech-heavy, often 1+ years; free job-seeker access only. |
| Wellfound | Startup operations/analytics; India-eligible remote | Candidate profile/applications are free; don't assume global remote means India eligible. |
| Internshala | Full-time jobs only | Reject internships, paid training and below-target salary maxima. |
| Unstop | Relevant full-time early-career opportunities | Exclude competitions, courses, internships and application fees. |

- Sender-domain support does not prove that an account is subscribed or that a platform supplies a reliable alert. Only enable native notifications actually offered by your account.
- Login/CAPTCHA-only destinations can be sent as optional phone actions **only after** independent fresh employer evidence and suitability checks. Unknown dates never become qualified actions.
- Naukri is not supported and must not be added to this label/integration.
